I want the blog to look as good as the sites that win on Awwwards. Looking at the 2025–2026 winners, one thing they share is **a single clear art direction**, and that idea still holds up as a still image, before any motion.

## Four ideas

1. **Ink and paper.** Warm paper, ink that spreads as you scroll, Vietnamese tone marks dropping into place as a title appears.
2. **Signal.** A CRT and ASCII world where text decodes itself into titles.
3. **Rhythm.** Type that moves to a beat; titles stretch and stack like a score.
4. **Knowledge constellation.** Every post is a star, topics connect them, and a click flies you to the post.

## How to score them

Awwwards scores four criteria with fixed weights. The final score is a weighted average:

$$
S = 0.4\,D + 0.3\,U + 0.2\,C + 0.1\,N
$$

where $D$ is design, $U$ usability, $C$ creativity and $N$ content. Design and usability make up $70\%$, so a beautiful blog that is hard to read still loses points.

## Next

All four versions have the full feature set: two languages, search, RSS, comments. I will try each one on my phone and then pick one.
