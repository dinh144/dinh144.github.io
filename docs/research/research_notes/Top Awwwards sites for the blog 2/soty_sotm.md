## What is the highest jury score on record in this window, and what do 8+ sites share?

### Takeaway
No site is 10/10. Highest overall jury score seen: Lando Norris 8.18 (SOTY 2025); Bruno's Portfolio 8.11 is second. Historical SOTY ceiling per a third-party teardown index: Prometheus Fuels 8.40 (2021), Lusion v3 8.25 (2023), Pioneer 8.18 (2020), Lando Norris 8.18, Igloo 7.92, KPR 7.98. Individual jurors do give 10/10/10/10 (e.g. Nick Chukreiev on Lando Norris), but the weighted mean (Design 40, Usability 30, Creativity 20, Content 10) averages jurors in 7s and 8s.

### Cited Findings
- Scoring weights: Design 40%, Usability 30%, Creativity 20%, Content 10%; Dev Award has six equal sub-scores. — [Lando Norris page](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/lando-norris)
- Lando Norris juror scores range from 7.00 to 10.00; Awwwards Users (community) gave 8.86. — [page](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/lando-norris)
- Prior SOTY scores: 2021 Prometheus Fuels 8.40; 2023 Lusion v3 8.25; 2025 Lando Norris 8.18; 2020 Pioneer 8.18; 2022 KPR 7.98; 2024 Igloo 7.92. — [rickcreates](https://rickcreates.com/year) (third-party; Lando Norris and Igloo match Awwwards pages)
- Only the two 8+ sites in my sample: Lando Norris (8.18) and Bruno's Portfolio (8.11). Both are personal/brand-of-a-person sites, both use WebGL/3D + GSAP, both have Creativity scores 8.6-8.7 and Usability 7.8-7.9 (highest usability in sample) with community scores 8.9-9.7. — pages above
- Sample Usability is the weakest jury axis for nearly every winner (7.1-7.9), Creativity the strongest (7.3-8.7). — pages above
- 25 of 31 SOTY winners carry a 3D/WebGL/Three.js tag; exceptions include Don't Board Me (graphic design + storytelling only) and the Pangram Pangram Foundry type foundry. — [EXHIBITA](https://exhibita.design/canon.html) (third-party)

### Inferences
- A 2026-11-09 SOTD at 7.6 is a normal SOTD-to-SOTM level score, so the realistic target for a blog is 7.5-8.0 overall.
- Usability is where a reading blog can out-score showpiece sites: those with high Usability (Bruno 7.83, Lando 7.9, Siena 7.61) pair spectacle with obvious navigation.
- Dev Award: winners lose points on Accessibility (6.0-7.6), Semantics (6.6-7.4) and Markup (6.4-7.6) while Animations hits 8-9.6. A blog with proper semantic HTML, WCAG-level accessibility and an Animations score near 9 would beat the typical Dev profile (Messenger's 8.21 is the best seen, driven by WPO 8.8 and Animations 9.0).

- No page listing "highest scores of all time" found; the 8.40 Prometheus figure is from a third-party index, not re-verified on Awwwards.

## Which winners are personal / portfolio / editorial and what can a reading blog borrow?

### Takeaway
Most relevant: Bruno's Portfolio (personal dev portfolio), David Whyte Experience (poetry, long-form scroll), Ponpon Mania (independent authors, illustration-led narrative), GQ x AP Extraordinary Lab and Tracing Art (editorial/content architecture), Anime.js (developer docs), Lando Norris (person site). Note: no SOTM winner in this window is a plain text-first blog; the closest long-reading analogues are David Whyte and Tracing Art.

### Cited Findings (borrowable ideas are INFERRED from tags/elements; see Inferences)
- Louis Paquet won Independent of the Year 2025 (MindMarket is his SOTM, Dec 2025). — [annual awards](https://www.awwwards.com/annual-awards/winners), [SOTM list](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/sites_of_the_month/)
- Awwwards elements highlighted on winners reflect what jurors notice: Igloo "Content Page" and footer links; Renaissance Edition 404 page, intro animation, section transitions; Don't Board Me custom 404 and pricing page; Siena dynamic menu and "Copy design"/contact type lettering. — pages above
- Lando Norris is built in Webflow + GSAP (not hand-rolled WebGL framework only) and still reached SOTY; Ponpon Mania, Floema and GQ/AP use Nuxt.js; Bruno uses Three.js + GSAP. — pages above
- Dev Award Animations scores: Igloo 9.60, Messenger 9.00, Ponpon Mania 9.00, Anime.js 9.00 (the animation-library site). — pages above

### Inferences
- Borrow (Lando Norris): one strong two-colour palette (#D2FF00 on #111112) plus a single recurring motif (helmet) tying all pages; cursor-reactive fragments in the hero.
- Borrow (Bruno): personality in the portfolio itself, but keep a plain content path for reading; his Accessibility 6.60 / Semantics 7.00 shows the gap a blog can close.
- Borrow (David Whyte / Tracing Art): long-form text paced by illustrated scroll sections and content architecture; for a blog, apply the pacing to article headers only.
- Borrow (Renaissance Edition, Don't Board Me, Igloo): polished 404 page, footer, and content page are judged separately as "elements".
- Borrow (Anime.js): a solo developer author winning SOTM with a docs site -> a dev blog with highly polished micro-animation and code-example UI is a credible target.
