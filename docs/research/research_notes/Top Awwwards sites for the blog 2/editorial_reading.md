# Award-level editorial / reading-first sites 2024-2026 (round 2, excludes the 15 already-studied sites)

Method note. Firecrawl ran out of credits mid-run, so I switched to Exa (award pages) and to curl through the sandbox proxy (page HTML and CSS, then a small probe script: fonts, max-widths, reduced-motion rules, libraries). The shared Playwright browser was being driven by another researcher (it navigated away mid-call), so I did not use it and have NO computed-style measurements or screenshots. Typographic numbers below are therefore either read from CSS (SEEN in CSS) or INFERRED. Article pages were probed only where a URL was found; where I probed a landing page instead, I say so. Evidence tags: SEEN = fetched page/CSS, INFERRED = my reasoning, GAP = not found.

## Which reading-first sites won awards in 2024-2026, and with what scores?

### Takeaway
Awwwards scores for reading-first sites are modest (SOTD 7.1 to 7.95; Developer Award 7.2 to 7.5), because the jury weights design 40 percent and rewards motion; the strongest "reading" credentials are on Webby/ADC/ASME side (Quanta, Long Lead, i-D). I found 11 usable references (table), of which 5 have a probed live page. Most Awwwards entries in the Magazine/Blog category right now are Honorable Mentions (no score published on the listing).

## How do they put motion/immersion into article pages while keeping body text readable?

### Takeaway
The winners keep the text column static and put motion in the frame around it: progress indicators, a changing masthead mark, image overlaps between text blocks, chapter transitions, and between-article sequencing. None of the probed sites animate the body glyphs themselves.

### Cited Findings (per-site design notes)
**1. Aaino** (aaino.com; Nuxt, Prismic, Tailwind). SEEN in HTML: `<html lang="en">`, Nuxt app, 354 Prismic references, Barba/swUp strings present once or twice (not confirmed as page-transition library). SEEN in CSS: display ABC Arizona Flare, body ABC Arizona Sans, body paragraph class weight 300, line-height 1.57; max-width values 768/1024/1440/1920 px (container breakpoints, no `ch` measure found). Motion per press: wordmark swaps to a dotted "A" on article pages and the dot orbits the A as you scroll, finishing below it at the end of the article (a progress indicator and reading-length marker); in text-heavy pieces images overlap and punctuate the text "as if turning a page"; at the end an article hands you to the next article in a curated sequence; the homepage is the current "Edit" (issue) as a visual table of contents; archive filters by section or edit. Decimal: motion "on loading, scrolling, reading an article... small moments of delight without being distracting." Reduced-motion: no `prefers-reduced-motion` string in the inlined CSS or in 12 of 20 JS chunks I fetched (partial check; GAP on the rest). — [aaino article page, fetched](https://aaino.com/article/bella-freud-on-dressing), [Brand Identity](https://the-brandidentity.com/project/w-e-studio-and-decimals-lyrical-editorial-platform-for-aaino-reads-like-a-magazine-but-only-better), [Decimal](https://decimalstudios.com/projects/aaino), [It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/w-e-studio-aaino-publication-project-170325)

**2. Claude on Mars** (anthropic.com/features; Next.js). SEEN in CSS: `--grid-reading-max-width: 720px` used as `--text-column-max-width`; also 65ch/75ch/80ch rules; body line-height 150% appears 32 times; `text-wrap: pretty`; heavy `font-feature-settings` (322 hits); sans + serif + mono tokens (`--anthropic-sans/-serif/-mono`) plus JetBrains Mono; 40 `prefers-reduced-motion` rules, and the sampled ones use the `(prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)` opt-in pattern (transitions on nav popups, hero-nav slide-away `body:has([data-hero-nav=away])`). Footnote/TOC/progress class names exist in the CSS. Motion elements per Awwwards: Earth-to-Mars animation, rover UI videos, risograph-scanned images, nav-camera gallery. Page transitions: none found (no view-transition CSS). — [live](https://www.anthropic.com/features/claude-on-mars), [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/claude-on-mars)

**3. JOSHUA'S Magazine** (joshuas.io; WordPress block theme, custom plugin `joshuas-central`). SEEN in HTML/CSS (probed an article, /brownlee-brothers-brotherhood-beyond/): `lang="en-US"`; Lenis referenced twice and Three.js twice in the HTML; Swiper via jsDelivr; CSS contains `view-transition` rules (5 hits) and `text-wrap: balance`. Its `prefers-reduced-motion` rules (8 in CSS) are WordPress core block defaults (image fade-in, lightbox, nav menu, cover parallax set to scroll), not site-specific: I saw no custom reduced-motion rule. Distinctive: a "day-light-switcher" block (frontend script `day-light-switcher-frontend.js`) matching the awarded Morning/Day/Evening/Night colour transition; layout and scroll transitions are listed as awarded elements. — [article page](https://joshuas.io/brownlee-brothers-brotherhood-beyond/), [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/joshuas-magazine)

**4. Quanta Magazine "Unraveling of Space-Time"** (quantamagazine.org; WordPress-derived, 21 `wp-content` hits; probed the issue landing page, not a single article). SEEN in CSS: Merriweather (serif body), Noe Display (headlines), Pangram (UI sans); `text-wrap: pretty`; `font-feature-settings`/`font-variant` rules; class names `progress`, `progress-bar`, `progress__time` (reading-time progress) and `toc-links`/`toc-block`; many `max-width` values at 33.99em, 35rem, 65.99em (a narrow text column, exact value INFERRED ~ 35rem since the first is a breakpoint style). No `prefers-reduced-motion` and no `view-transition` in the fetched CSS (the site is mostly static; its immersion comes from custom art and video per piece). — [fetched page](https://www.quantamagazine.org/the-unraveling-of-space-time-20240925/), [award post](https://www.quantamagazine.org/quantanews/quanta-wins-2025-national-magazine-award-for-best-single-topic-issue-and-webby-peoples-voice-award/)

**5. Home of the Brave** (homeofthebrave.longlead.com; headless WordPress/GraphQL + Next.js per the makers). SEEN in CSS: Druk and Druk XCond display, Trade Gothic and Trade Gothic Condensed for UI, Rosart (serif) for text; no `prefers-reduced-motion` in the 72 KB CSS I fetched (GAP: JS not checked). Design per CommArts: immersive, clean, bold, military-garb inspired; seven Parts are the primary navigation, Credits and Subscribe secondary; the 35,000-word piece "made digestible". — [live](https://homeofthebrave.longlead.com/), [CommArts](https://www.commarts.com/webpicks/home-of-the-brave)

**6. CITIUS Mag** (citiusmag.com; Next.js). SEEN in CSS: ABC Monument Grotesk (+ Mono), Adieu (display), GT Alpina and GT Alpina Condensed (serif text); one `prefers-reduced-motion` rule; max-widths 48rem and 65rem. Awarded "Article Page Design" element exists but I did not probe an article URL (GAP). — [live](https://www.citiusmag.com/), [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/citius-mag)

**7. Into The Amazon** (Gladeye). Awwwards tags: Infinite Scroll, Data Visualization, Storytelling; elements: LiDAR fly-through, map fly-through, dynamic map menu (a map-driven navigation). The domain I guessed returned an empty page, so no CSS/stack data (GAP). — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/into-the-amazon)

**8. i-D, ESPN Pat Tillman, LMI Van Gogh**: no probe possible (i-d.co is blocked by the sandbox allowlist; others not attempted). Only award facts are known (see above). GAP for typography/motion.

**9. Hearst Exhibit 2026** (Awwwards SOTD 2 Aug 2026, 7.21, DEV 7.46: animations 8.00): a photography exhibition, so reading is not the main job; useful only for its elements (Paper-curl WebGL transition, list/grid gallery) and palette of just #252525 / #E3E3E3. Tags GSAP, WebGL, Vercel. — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/hearst-exhibit-2026)

### Inferences
- The pattern across Aaino, Quanta and Home of the Brave: the text column is quiet; the signature element is a progress/identity mark (orbiting dot, progress bar, chapter nav) that costs almost nothing in motion budget, so it can be kept in reading mode as a static progress bar.
- Joshua's shows the risk: a Lenis-plus-Three stack with only WordPress-core reduced-motion handling means its custom motion (probably) ignores the preference; this is INFERRED from CSS only.
- Anthropic's `no-preference` opt-in gating is the safest default for the blog's "motion only when motionAllowed()" contract.

### Gaps
- No site had its JS audited for `matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion)')`; only CSS was checked.
- No computed type scale (px size, measure in characters) was measured live because the browser was unavailable.

## How do they handle footnotes, sidenotes, TOC, and long pages on phones?

### Takeaway
I found no award-winning site with true sidenotes. TOC and progress exist as class names at Quanta and Anthropic; the most concrete mobile pattern is Aaino's responsive layout with the same progress mark, and Harvard Gazette's accessibility-first rebuild.

### Cited Findings
- Quanta CSS contains `toc-links`, `toc-block` and `progress__time` classes; Anthropic's CSS contains footnote, toc and progress class names (names only, behaviour not observed). — [Quanta](https://www.quantamagazine.org/the-unraveling-of-space-time-20240925/), [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/features/claude-on-mars)
- Aaino navigates long reading by sequencing: finishing an article routes to the next article in a curated order; the "Edit" front page is a visual table of contents. — [Brand Identity](https://the-brandidentity.com/project/w-e-studio-and-decimals-lyrical-editorial-platform-for-aaino-reads-like-a-magazine-but-only-better)
- Home of the Brave breaks a 35,000-word piece into seven Parts as the primary navigation. — [CommArts](https://www.commarts.com/webpicks/home-of-the-brave)
- Harvard Gazette: Human Made built it accessibility-first with the university's digital accessibility team, using Gutenberg blocks and "scrollytelling" blocks and quizzes. — [Webby Crafted with Code](https://www.webbyawards.com/crafted-with-code/the-harvard-gazette/)
- Salud (Studio Size, not an awarded site as far as I found) describes semantic HTML, strong contrast and keyboard navigation as foundational, off-white and charcoal instead of pure white/black, variable fonts, minimal purposeful motion. — [Medium, 19 May 2025](https://medium.com/studiosize/salud-designing-a-digital-magazine-that-feels-like-print-3eef1afe44d2)

### Inferences
- For a dev blog, sidenotes on desktop and inline-expanding notes on phones would be differentiated from every site found here.

### Gaps
- Mobile behaviour of footnotes was not observed on any site (no browser).

## Are there award-winning sites with diacritic-heavy typography (Vietnamese or similar)?

### Takeaway
I found no award-winning Vietnamese-language long-form reading site. Vietnamese studios do win Awwwards (a Vietnam country list exists) but mostly portfolios and agency sites.

### Cited Findings
- Awwwards Vietnam list: Huy Phan Vol.2 (SOTD 14 Oct 2026, DEV Award, No-code Honors), Eislab (SOTD 25 May 2025, DEV), NIKI Studio (SOTD 3 Feb 2025, DEV), Lumen Artspace (HM 2 Jul 2025), plus many HMs; none is a reading site. — [Awwwards Vietnam](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/Vietnam/)
- Lumen Artspace (Vietnamese lacquer art gallery, Webflow + GSAP) reports CSS Site of the Day, FWA Site of the Day and Awwwards HM (self-reported by the studio); closest Vietnamese-content story-first award site. — [Contra case study](https://contra.com/p/M22qvsgv-lumen-artspace-heritage-in-motion-or-award-winning-website)
- Poé Magazine (Quychibeo, Behance, 4 Mar 2026) is a print Vietnamese-French poetry magazine that turns diacritics into headline treatment; not an awarded website. — [Behance](https://www.behance.net/gallery/245125573/Po-Magazine)
- Vietnam Airlines Heritage e-magazine (TELOS) is a bilingual Vietnamese/English publication site but no award is claimed in the source. — [TELOS](https://telos.vn/project/website-vietnam-airline-heritage/)
- La Libre's "Ukraine: a country mined" (French, Awwwards nominee, Josefin Sans, `hyphens` rules present in CSS) is a diacritic-using example but only a nominee. — [fetched](https://dossiers.lalibre.be/ukraine-mines/), [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/ukraine-a-country-mined)

### Inferences
- A bilingual Vietnamese/English reading theme with tested stacked diacritics would be unusual even among awarded sites; none of the probed font stacks (Arizona, Alpina, Merriweather, Druk) were checked for the Vietnamese subset, so do not assume they support it.

### Gaps
- No Vietnamese subset coverage was verified for any of the fonts above.

## Concrete things a dev blog could borrow (summary)
- Aaino: a masthead mark that doubles as a reading-progress indicator and finishes at article end; "next article" chosen by editorial sequence; images overlapping between paragraphs.
- Anthropic: `--grid-reading-max-width: 720px` as one token for the text column; gate every transition behind `(prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`; `text-wrap: pretty`.
- Quanta: serif body (Merriweather) plus display serif (Noe Display) plus UI sans, a progress bar with a reading-time label, a TOC block; motion kept in custom art, not text.
- Home of the Brave: split a long piece into numbered Parts as primary navigation.
- Joshua's: a time-of-day theme switch as a reading preference (as a user-controlled toggle; avoid mimicking its WordPress-core-only reduced-motion handling).
- Awwwards scoring lesson: DEV sub-scores include Accessibility and WPO, so the reading-mode and reduced-motion fallback are directly scoreable.
