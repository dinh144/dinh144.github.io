## Findings / strongly proven directions
- Game / explorable world: strongly proven (many SOTDs 7.5-7.95 with game, pixel, map or island navigation; plus the already-known Bruno Simon 8.11, Messenger 7.92).
- Anime/manga/cel: proven for comics and picture books (Ponpon Mania, Black Dog, Black Messiah), not for "anime city".
- Vietnamese: proven at HM and studio level (Lumen Artspace, Banh mi Viet Nam, Eislab, NIKI, monopo saigon); no SOTD found that is itself a Vietnamese-traditional-aesthetic world.
- Chinese ink and Japanese wabi-sabi: proven mostly as HM/low-7s SOTD; usually dark/thin type and no long-form reading. Not a proven combination with a calm reading panel.
- Gap: none of these shows article-length reading. The reading panel must be our own design (paper tone, 16-18 px, high contrast).
- Awwwards is thin on Vietnamese-diacritic body text; none of the captures tested long Vietnamese paragraphs.
