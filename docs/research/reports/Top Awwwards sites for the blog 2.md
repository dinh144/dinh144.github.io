# No Awwwards site scores 10/10; 8.18 is the ceiling

**No site in the 2024-2026 Awwwards window scores 10/10. The highest overall jury score found is Lando Norris at 8.18, which was Site of the Year 2025, and the second is Bruno Simon's portfolio at 8.11.** ([Lando Norris](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/lando-norris), [Bruno's Portfolio](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/brunos-portfolio)) Individual jurors do hand out 10s, but the weighted mean (Design 40%, Usability 30%, Creativity 20%, Content 10%) lands in the 7s and 8s. Personal-site Sites of the Day cluster at 7.2-7.8, and the only personal project above 8.0 is Sleep Well Creative at 8.03, a personal essay site rather than a portfolio. The four themes' internal scores of 7.3-7.63 therefore already sit inside the Site-of-the-Day band; the realistic target is 7.6-8.0 overall plus a Developer Award of 7.5 or more, not 10. Three findings matter for the lab. First, Usability is the weakest jury axis on almost every winner (6.9-7.9), so a blog that reads well can out-score showpieces on that axis. Second, Accessibility is the lowest Developer sub-score on nearly every winner (6.0-7.8), so the reading mode and reduced-motion fallback are directly scoreable. Third, the best-crafted personal developer blogs keep prose calm and put motion inside article demos, and none of them is bilingual. Most live sites could not be opened from the sandbox, so every stack and motion claim below is marked as verified or inferred, and the gaps are listed at the end for the awwwards-mcp follow-up.

**Evidence tags.** V-A means the figures were read on a fetched Awwwards page. V-L means the live page, CSS or a repository was fetched or measured in a browser on 2026-11-09. V-T means a third-party teardown, such as rickcreates.com, which measures in a real browser. I means inferred. G means a gap.

## The ceiling is 8.18, and no winner clears it

Awwwards jury scoring weights Design 40%, Usability 30%, Creativity 20% and Content 10%. The Developer Award averages six equal sub-scores: Semantics, Animations, Accessibility, WPO, Responsive and Markup ([Lando Norris page](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/lando-norris)). A third-party index puts earlier Site-of-the-Year scores at Prometheus Fuels 8.40 (2021), Lusion v3 8.25 (2023), Pioneer 8.18 (2020), KPR 7.98 (2022) and Igloo 7.92 (2024) ([rickcreates](https://rickcreates.com/year)). Only the Lando Norris and Igloo figures were re-checked against Awwwards, so the 8.40 is unverified. Individual jurors do award 10 on every axis, for example on Lando Norris, where juror scores range from 7.00 to 10.00 and the community vote is 8.86. Jury splits are wide: on one 2026 portfolio individual jurors ranged from 6.1 to 9.6. The system discards the three scores furthest from the average ([Liuhuo Stack summary](https://stack.liuhuo.org/en/discover/awwwards)). The practical reading is that a 10/10 weighted average does not occur, and an 8.0 overall is already Site-of-the-Month territory.

## Which eight references to study first with awwwards-mcp

The ranking weights how much each site can close an open gap in the lab: motion behaviour under reduced-motion, mobile fallback, scroll maps and reading-column measurements. The theme mapping uses the four concept names only, because the concept briefs were not part of this research, so it is inferred (I): ink for typography and reading, signal for crisp UI and interaction restraint, rhythm for scroll pacing and sound, constellation for 3D and spatial navigation.

1. **landonorris.com** (8.18, SOTY 2025). Record motion on the hero cursor-fragment effect and run analyze_scroll_map on the "Graphic Scroll" and hall of fame. Verify whether a reduced-motion or mobile path exists. Informs rhythm and signal; the single-motif two-colour palette also informs ink.
2. **Sleep Well Creative** (8.03, the highest personal site). Measure the chapter background flip, the letter-by-letter title reveal and the scroll map across 53 desktop screens, and check what reduced motion does (reported as ignored). Informs ink and rhythm.
3. **bruno-simon.com** (8.11, personal developer portfolio, Three.js and GSAP). Record the drivable world, mobile controls and the loading path; ask how a plain reading path could coexist. Informs constellation.
4. **Jesper Landberg** (SOTD 7.73, Developer 8.17). Record the ribbon and black-hole overlay, the mobile vertical-card fallback and the focus order; its Developer profile is the best personal one. Informs constellation and signal.
5. **Cyd Stumpel** (Developer 7.74, A11y 7.8). Capture the CSS scroll-driven animations against the GSAP fallback and the blog article page; this is the closest to the lab's motion contract. Informs signal and ink.
6. **Messenger** (Developer SOTY, 8.21). Record character navigation, WebSocket-driven NPCs and the WPO behaviour on throttled mobile. Informs constellation.
7. **Aaino** (Honorable Mention). Record the orbiting-dot progress mark and image overlaps on a long article, and finish the JS check for reduced-motion handling. Informs ink and rhythm.
8. **Maxime Heckel's blog** (shader canvases per article). Record one article with several canvases to see how R3F motion behaves under reduced motion (not tested yet). Informs constellation and ink.
9. **Igloo** (SOTY 2024, Animations 9.6). Run analyze_scroll_map on the camera descent; its 6.6 Semantics and Accessibility scores mark the pitfall to avoid. Informs constellation and rhythm.
10. **Brandon Yasin** (Developer 7.77). Record the time-of-day palette and draggable clock, and test whether the editorial menu stays usable on phones. Informs rhythm and ink.

## Gaps that the follow-up must close

Most live sites could not be opened from the sandbox, including bruno-simon.com, animejs.com and landonorris.com. Stacks for Awwwards winners therefore come from Awwwards tags, and typography families, easing and durations, sound, cursor handling, page-transition implementation, mobile behaviour and `prefers-reduced-motion` handling are unverified for every Site of the Year and Site of the Month. Only the developer blogs (Playwright, 390 px: no horizontal overflow on any) and a few editorial sites (CSS probes) were measured live, and only CSS was checked for reduced motion, not `matchMedia` in JS. Sub-scores are missing for several Sites of the Month and for Pacome Pertant's Developer Award, and the 8.40 Prometheus Fuels score is third-party and unverified. Awwwards does not publish jury comments, so every claim about why a site scored as it did is inference. No performance data (Lighthouse, INP) was collected, and the Godly, Land-book, siteinspire and Minimal Gallery collections were not checked for the developer blogs. Footnote, TOC and sidenote behaviour on phones was never observed. The 2026 annual awards are not decided as of 2026-11-09, so the "highest on record" claim covers only what is listed above.

## Conclusion

The research reframes the target. A 10/10 does not exist, the ceiling is about 8.2, and the lab's 7.3-7.63 sits where Sites of the Day cluster, so the remaining gain is not a better hero effect. It is the two axes winners leave weak: Usability (6.9-7.9) and Accessibility (6.0-7.8). Cyd Stumpel's 7.8 Accessibility and the many 7.0 or lower scores on spectacle sites show that a documented reading mode, visible focus, real landmarks and a reduced-motion path move the Developer score without costing Creativity. Winners at 7.6 or more each rest on a single metaphor that is the interface, while the best developer blogs keep prose quiet and spend their motion budget inside demos.

Two positions follow. A bilingual Vietnamese/English reading theme with tested stacked diacritics has no craft-level precedent among awarded or celebrated sites, so it is a differentiator rather than a risk, provided the fonts are verified for the Vietnamese subset. And "immersive motion on every page" is not what the strongest reference points do on text pages, so a defensible policy is Emil Kowalski's rules (purpose, frequency, under 300 ms) for page chrome, with long durations reserved for hero and chapter moments and everything stopping when `motionAllowed()` turns false.
